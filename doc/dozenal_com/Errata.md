# Errata — *Proposal for the Universal Unit System* (univunit-e.pdf, 2002)

The PDF is kept unaltered, so that it remains identical to the copies archived
by third parties. Corrections are listed here instead, grouped by the date on
which they were recorded.

## 2019-03-21

First published as `Errata.html` beside the PDF on asahi-net, where its file
date can still be checked; `Errata.html` in this directory is that file,
unchanged. Its three items, reproduced:

- p. 10, §3.2.2 — "Written as *rm*rad<sup>2</sup>" → "Written as rad<sup>2</sup>"
- p. 13, §3.4 — "Derived unit of work" → "Derived unit of power"
- p. 23, B.4 — Figure 1 and the expressions of B.3 are quoted from different
  manuscripts. For this reason the symbols of the two do not correspond.

## 2026-10-01

- p. 23, B.4, Figure 1 — a specific case of the third 2019 item. In Figure 1,
  *ρ* and *j* denote surface densities, whereas *ρ* in B.3 (which reproduces
  the *Parity* text) is a volume density. Read "e. quantity density" as
  "e. surface quantity density" and "e. current density" as "e. surface current
  density": the ×area arrows to the quantity *Q* and the ÷length arrow from the
  current *I* close only for surface densities, as the Japanese original
  (*Parity* **12**(4), 1997, Fig. 1) reads: 表面電荷密度, 表面電流密度.

  (The current densities need no correction: Figure 1's *j* and B.3's bold **J**
  are different quantities and already carry different symbols.)
